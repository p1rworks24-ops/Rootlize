What you can do

Find. Narrow. Act. Automate.

Rootlize is the workspace for files already on your PC — not another cloud library.

  1. Find

    Basic Search, OCR, and Meaning Search help you find the images you need.

  2. Narrow

    Refine a large folder into the results that matter.

  3. Act

    Tag, favorite, rename, and move files without leaving the workspace.

  4. Automate

    Turn repeatable work into reusable workflows.

On your PC

Use the folder you already have.

Point Rootlize at an existing local folder. Your images stay there. Rootlize does not move the library into a Rootlize cloud.

Stays on this PC

Basic Search, OCR, and bundled Meaning Search run locally. Sign-in does not upload the library.

Needs sign-in and internet

The Prototype opens on a sign-in screen. Ask AI uses a cloud provider after you agree, and may send image contents.

Privacy Policy Terms

Deeper capabilities

Search by meaning. Ask in your own words. Repeat the work.

The workspace goes beyond a search box — once you have a set, you can ask, organize, and repeat the work.

Meaning Search

Find images by what they show. The model ships with the app — no extra setup.

Ask AI

Describe find-and-organize work in your own words. Needs sign-in and the internet.

Automation

Save a repeated find-and-organize flow, then run it again on the same kind of local work.
